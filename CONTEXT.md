# Competitive curling

The competitive circuit as a circuit regular follows it: open bonspiels and championships.

## Language

### Who plays

**Circuit regular**:
A fan who follows competitive curling all season and comes for the scores, the linescores, the standings, and the rankings. The person the site is designed for.
_Avoid_: casual fan, user, big-event fan

**Big-event fan**:
A fan who shows up for Grand Slams, national championships, Worlds, and the Olympics.
_Avoid_: casual

**Person**:
An athlete. Their identity is independent of how their name is spelled on a page.
_Avoid_: curler, player

**Skip**:
A role a Person holds on an Event entry or in a Game. The label fans read is often the skip's name. The role is not an identity.
_Avoid_: team, entry

**Team**:
One Discipline and the set of Persons on the main roster. That same set is the same Team at every Event. Replacing one of those Persons makes a new Team. An alternate is not part of the set.
_Avoid_: event entry, franchise, skip

**Event entry**:
One side in one Event, shown by a label such as the skip's surname. The same Team at another Event is a new Event entry.
_Avoid_: team, lineup, side

### Competition

**Circuit**:
Open bonspiels and championships. Club-league play is outside it.
_Avoid_: tour, league

**Event**:
One held competition, with a title and a date range. The 2025 Scotties and the 2023 Tim Hortons Brier are Events.
_Avoid_: spiel, tournament, bonspiel, competition, league, event series

**Event series**:
The recurring name an Event belongs to, such as the Brier or the Scotties. One year of it is an Event.
_Avoid_: season, tour

**Discipline**:
The format of an Event or a Team: Men, Women, Mixed, Open, Wheelchair, Mixed Doubles, Wheelchair Mixed Doubles, Two-Person Stick, or Triples.
_Avoid_: division, gender, category

**Eligibility**:
An age or level limit on an Event, such as Junior, Senior, College, or Club Championship. It is not a Discipline.
_Avoid_: division, category

**Draw**:
The Games in one Event that share a listed time.
_Avoid_: session, round, slate

**Venue**:
A building or club where Games are played, with a name and a city.
_Avoid_: location, place

### Results

**Game**:
One contest between two Event entries.
_Avoid_: match, draw

**Score**:
The current total for each side of a Game. This is what a fan scans across many Events.
_Avoid_: linescore, running score

**Linescore**:
The end-by-end scores of one Game, and the listed final. An end that was not played is absent.
_Avoid_: scoreline, box score

**Standing**:
An Event entry's wins, losses, and rank inside one Event.
_Avoid_: ranking, world ranking

**Playoff**:
The games that decide an Event after the preliminary round.
_Avoid_: bracket, knockout
